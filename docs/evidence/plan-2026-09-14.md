## Track D — the second tier, built on both sides and never mounted

18 endpoints, 5 API namespaces with zero component callers, and 8 finished result cards
that nothing imports: `feed` (opportunity/loss scans), `reconciliation` (cross-source),
`temporal` (lag analysis), `exploration` (autonomous investigation), `data_graph` (metric
catalogue), half of `data_validation`, and `WrongDataModal` — the entire "this data is
wrong" investigation flow, three endpoints and three components.

This is the largest block of shipped-but-unreachable product in the repository. It is
**not** a fix; it is a product decision about what the product is. Decomposed, not built,
until that decision is made:

| # | Item | Question that must be answered first |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | `WrongDataModal` → mount it on the thumbs-down path | is investigation the answer to "wrong data", or is the canned chat message? |
| D2 | `feed` / `reconciliation` / `temporal` / `exploration` | do these belong in chat as cards, or on their own panel? |
| D3 | `data_graph` metric catalogue | is this the semantic layer the roadmap names, or a second one? |
